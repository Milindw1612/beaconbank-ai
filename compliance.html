India AI Regulatory Landscape

RBI & National AI Compliance Map

India's banking sector is moving fast on AI while regulators move to match it. This page summarizes how Beacon Bank's plan maps to the applicable frameworks. The full clause-by-clause mapping, lifecycle-stage detail, and source citations are walked through live in a technical deep-dive.

Regulatory Landscape at a Glance

RBI FREE-AI Framework

Final
Reserve Bank of India · Committee report, 2025

Guiding principles and structured recommendations across an Innovation Enablement and Risk Mitigation dual framework for every RBI-regulated entity.

MeitY AI Governance Guidelines

Final
Ministry of Electronics & IT · 2025

A lightweight, adaptive national AI governance approach across all sectors, built on existing law rather than a new AI statute.

SEBI AI/ML Guidelines

Consultation
Securities and Exchange Board of India

Governance, bias mitigation, investor protection and risk controls for AI/ML in securities markets.

IRDAI AI Governance

In Progress
Insurance Regulatory & Development Authority

A working group drafting the insurance sector's first formal AI governance framework.

CERT-In AI-Era Blueprint

Advisory
Indian Computer Emergency Response Team

A blueprint mandating rapid critical-vulnerability patching, zero-trust and secure-by-design controls as AI accelerates attack speed.

DPDP Act 2023 & Rules

Statute
Ministry of Electronics & IT

India's data protection law — consent, data-principal rights, fiduciary obligations, and breach notification.
