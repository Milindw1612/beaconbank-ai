Risk & Governance Framework

Governance — Human Control, Data Protection & Regulatory Alignment

How Beacon Bank's flagship agentic AI use cases stay accountable: what's automated vs. what always requires a human decision. The detailed threshold tables, PII mechanisms, and audit specifications are walked through live in a technical deep-dive.

Human-in-the-Loop, by Use Case

MSME Credit CAM Prep

Read-only bureau/AA integration. Every recommendation is a draft — a human credit officer holds final lending authority on every application, no exceptions.

Fraud Detection

Auto-freeze only fires above a governance-approved confidence threshold. Everything in the mid-confidence band routes to a human analyst before any account action.

Collections Recovery

Negotiation is bounded by pre-approved settlement bands. Anything outside the band, or any customer pushback, escalates to a human officer.

Design principle: in every use case, the agents draft, score, or recommend — they never hold unilateral authority over money movement, a fraud confirmation, or a lending decision.
Data Protection & PII
Zero-tolerance rule: customer name, PAN, Aadhaar, account/card number, and outstanding balances never enter a model prompt in raw form. Tokenisation, encryption, and prompt-scrubbing are proven in code across all 3 pilots — the specific mechanisms are walked through live.

Go-Live Validation Gates
Why this exists: none of the pilots have run in live production yet, so there's no real measured accuracy number to show a Board today. Rather than assert one, each pilot has a concrete, measurable pass/fail threshold it must clear in a shadow-mode trial before it's allowed to go live. The specific thresholds and measurement methodology are walked through live.
  • A failed gate means the pilot does not go live — it returns to the build/tune phase for that specific metric
  • A designated risk/model-validation function reviews any failure before a re-test or redesign
  • Every gate outcome is logged, not just the passing ones
