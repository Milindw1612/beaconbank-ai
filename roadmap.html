Enterprise AI Roadmap — Day 0 to Year 3

How Beacon Bank scales from 3 proven flagship pilots into a bank-wide, governed AI operating model — phased across the first 90 days, 6 months, 12 months, and out to 3 years. This is the headline shape; the detailed operating model, workforce plan, and regulatory mapping are walked through live.

Industry Context — Where Indian Banking Stands on AI

Large Private Banks

Several have publicly discussed GenAI/AI initiatives over the past couple of years, mostly in customer service, fraud detection, and internal productivity — largely as point solutions, not a governed enterprise operating model.

Public Sector Banks

Generally earlier-stage, often piloting through industry-coordinated or vendor-led initiatives rather than in-house agentic builds.

RBI's Own Framing

RBI's own committee reporting treats the sector as early-innings overall — a call to build responsible AI capability now, not a claim that any bank has already solved it.

The Opportunity Here

Most of the sector still sits at "one-off pilot." Moving to a governed, enterprise-scale operating model is a genuine differentiator, not catch-up.

Directional industry framing for Board context, not a competitive-intelligence claim about any named institution's internal capability.

Horizon Themes
0–3 Months

Foundation

Governance chartered, policy published, flagship pilots in build. The bank has a named AI accountability structure for the first time.

3–6 Months

First Production, First Assurance

The first flagship pilot goes live. Independent model validation and the first internal audit of AI governance both stand up in parallel.

6–12 Months

Full Pilot Wave + Readiness

All flagship pilots in production. A 3-lines-of-defense model is operational. A regulatory-readiness dry-run is completed before scaling further.

Year 2

Enterprise Scale-Up

A second wave of use cases across new business lines goes live on the same governed platform. The AI CoE becomes a self-serve capability, not a project team.

Year 3

Enterprise AI Maturity

Every major business line runs at least one governed agentic system. External certification is targeted. Governance, audit and reporting are business-as-usual.

Three-Year Vision

From a few pilots to an enterprise AI Factory

By Year 3, Beacon Bank runs agentic AI across every major business line — lending, fraud, collections, and more — on one governed platform, not a collection of one-off projects.

Every system carries a documented risk tier, an independent model validation record, and a traceable audit trail from day one — not retrofitted after a regulator asks. Governance keeps pace with scale: a 3-lines-of-defense AI risk model, quarterly Board reporting, and an annual Responsible AI transparency report are business-as-usual, not a one-time exercise.

The target external validation point is an AI management system certification, benchmarked internally against RBI's FREE-AI Framework — proof the bank's AI program is governed to a standard a regulator, an auditor, and a customer can all independently verify.
