  • Approve the Enterprise Agentic AI & AI Agent Governance Policy Establishes risk tiers, autonomy limits, and Board/committee accountability before any further scale-up — see Governance
  • Charter the Board AI & Technology Committee and formally appoint a Chief AI Officer Names the accountable executive and oversight body
  • Approve the Go-Live Validation Gates as the pass/fail standard for all pilots before production launch Concrete, measurable thresholds — not yet-measured results — see Governance

Why This Is Safe to Approve
No agent holds unilateral authority over money movement, a fraud confirmation, or a lending decision.

Every flagship pilot is a draft-and-recommend design — a human credit officer, fraud analyst, or collections officer signs off on every consequential action. See Governance.

Why This Isn't Overselling
None of the pilots have run in production yet — so we're not showing a measured result we don't have.

Instead of an invented accuracy number, the Board is asked to approve concrete, measurable go/no-go thresholds each pilot must clear before launch. See Governance.
