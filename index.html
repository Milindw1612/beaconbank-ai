Consensus pick — appeared across every model run. MSME loan file assembly (GST returns, AA bank feeds, ITRs, CIBIL, financials) takes several days per application against a large MSME lending book, capping origination velocity.
The Approach
A coordinated multi-agent system ingests financial documents, verifies against bureau and tax data, checks for cash-flow risk, and drafts a pre-populated credit memo — with a human credit officer always making the final call.

Consensus pick — appeared across most model runs. Fraud teams triage thousands of daily alerts across cards/UPI/IMPS; most are false positives. Manual investigation takes tens of minutes per case, delaying genuine fraud response.
The Approach
